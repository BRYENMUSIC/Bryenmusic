06 / About
Bryen is a singer, songwriter, producer, and writer. Alongside his own artistry, he has established himself as a versatile songwriter with extensive experience writing music for recognized artists across the industry. His creative work draws from R&B, pop, alternative, and rock, combining different influences to create a sound that is distinctly his own. As both an artist and songwriter, Bryen continues to develop his craft while creating music that reflects his growth, perspective, and creative vision.
